# Consent note for interviews

Read this to the participant, or send it with the email if they ask for written terms. It is a student-project note, not a university ethics-board form. If the supervisor or the university requires a formal consent form, use that form instead and keep this note as the plain-language summary.

## What we are doing

Students on the diploma project “Development of an Intelligent System for Maximizing Solar Energy Utilization at an Industrial Facility Using Battery Storage Systems” are collecting professional opinions about how industrial sites use solar generation and batteries. The purpose is to check whether the project’s problem statement and requirements match practice.

## What we will ask

Questions about roles, operating practice, constraints, available data, and what result would be trusted. We will not ask for passwords, customer lists, or identifiable meter files in the first conversation.

## What we will write down

A dated note in the project repository: organisation or a coarser description if you prefer, your role, and the points that affect requirements. The note should not include your home contact details, health information, or confidential site data.

## How the note is used

The team, the supervisor, and the diploma committee can read the repository. Notes may be quoted in the relevance section. Verified statements will be tied to the conversation. Everything else in the design documents stays labelled as an assumption.

## Your choice

The conversation is voluntary. You can skip a question, stop, or ask us to delete the note before the pre-defense tag. You can refuse to be named. A written reply to the short survey is also voluntary and will be labelled as a written response, not as a meeting.
