Replace the bracketed fields before sending. Use the student’s own name and university address. Do not send this text with “Member A” still in it.

Send one message per organisation in [contacts.md](contacts.md). Keep a copy of the date sent in [interview-log.md](interview-log.md).

Subject: Student question on solar and batteries at industrial sites (20–30 min)

Dear colleagues,

My name is [Student name]. I am a student at [University], working with [teammate names] on a diploma project supervised by [Supervisor name].

The project specifies a decision-support tool for one industrial facility that has photovoltaic generation and a battery. The tool would suggest a charge and discharge schedule for the next day so that more of the site’s solar energy is used on site. We are not selling equipment, and we are not asking for meter data in this first conversation.

I would like 20–30 minutes with an engineer or project lead who has seen how factories or commercial sites actually use solar and storage. If your organisation supplies equipment rather than operating a plant, an introduction to a customer’s energy manager would be just as useful.

I am trying to learn:

- what happens to surplus solar in the middle of the day;
- whether batteries are scheduled by a person, a fixed timetable, or a vendor controller;
- which problems matter in practice (export limits, evening load, demand peaks, battery wear, missing data);
- which result an energy manager would trust.

I can meet by phone or video in the next two weeks, at a time that suits you. If a call is difficult, I can send six short questions by email instead.

Thank you for considering this.

[Student name]
[University email]
[Phone]
