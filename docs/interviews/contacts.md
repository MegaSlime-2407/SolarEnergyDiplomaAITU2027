# Practitioner contacts

These are public business contacts for organisations that work with renewable energy, industrial batteries, or battery-storage projects in Kazakhstan. They are starting points for a 20–30 minute conversation. They are not a statement that any of them has agreed to talk, and they are not end users until an interview says so.

Prefer a written reply or a call with an engineer or project lead. Ask for an introduction to a factory energy manager when the contact builds or supplies equipment rather than operating a plant.

Send the message in [outreach-email.md](outreach-email.md). Record every attempt and every reply in [interview-log.md](interview-log.md). Do not put private mobile numbers, personal opinions, or site meter data into the Git repository.

## Who not to treat as the primary interview

Nazarbayev University’s Renewable Energy Laboratory (relab@nu.edu.kz) works mainly on solar materials, fuels, and hydrogen. That is a weak match for battery dispatch at a factory. Use it only if the four contacts above do not respond and the questions are rewritten for a research audience.

## Backup if a meeting is refused

If a contact will not take a call, offer the short written survey in [survey-backup.md](survey-backup.md). A survey is additional evidence. The pre-defense expectation is still three to five discussions, so a form reply alone should be labelled as a written response, not as an interview.
